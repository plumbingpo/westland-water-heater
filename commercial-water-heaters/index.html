Warning Signs to Watch For

Most tanks announce trouble weeks or months ahead with rumbling noises, rusty water, or hot showers that keep getting shorter. Keep an eye out for the following:

  • Fixtures that run too hot one minute and cool the next
  • Dish or hand sink temperatures drifting below what your inspector expects
  • Distant fixtures that take minutes to run warm
  • Rumbling, popping, or banging from a heavily used tank
  • The tank giving up in the middle of a rush
  • Equipment approaching the end of its expected service life
  • Energy costs climbing while usage stays flat
  • Rusty water or damp spots around the equipment

Water Heater Care Tips From Our Technicians

Routine maintenance is almost always cheaper and easier than an early replacement. Follow these tips from our technicians:

  • Check anodes on a schedule matched to your volume and water quality
  • Keep recirculation pumps and check valves in service to avoid long waits and wasted energy
  • Track temperatures at the dish machine and hand sinks as part of routine checks
  • Budget for replacement as equipment nears the end of its expected life
  • Set a flushing schedule based on usage; busy tanks collect sediment fast
  • Keep the area around the heaters clear for combustion air and service access
  • Have mixing valves checked so fixtures stay neither scalding nor lukewarm

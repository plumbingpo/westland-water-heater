Recognize These Water Heater Warning Signs?

Water heater trouble rarely announces itself politely. If you're dealing with any of these problems, call us today for expert help.

Hot Water Gone Cold

A tripped high-limit switch, a failed gas valve, or a dead igniter can all look like the same symptom. Real diagnosis beats parts-swapping every time.

Fast repair help

Water Around the Heater

A dripping temperature and pressure relief valve is a warning sign, not a nuisance. It usually means excess pressure or heat, often from a missing expansion tank.

Leak repair

Rumbling Tank, Rusty Water

Popping and rumbling mean sediment has built up on the bottom of the tank, where it traps heat, wastes energy, and wears out the steel faster.

Water heater maintenance

Aging Tank, Mounting Repairs

We size replacements by household demand and first-hour rating, not a guess, so the new heater keeps up with your busiest mornings from day one.

Water heater replacement

Tankless Error Codes

An error code on a tankless unit is the heater telling you what it doesn't like, often scale buildup, an ignition fault, or a flow sensor problem.

Tankless repair

Running Out of Hot Water

A growing family, a new soaking tub, or back-to-back showers can outrun a 40-gallon tank. A tankless unit heats water on demand, so it doesn't run out.

If the tank itself is leaking, repair is off the table, and a replacement typically runs $1,100 to $2,800 installed for a standard tank or $2,800 to $5,500 for tankless. We diagnose the problem first and give you a clear, upfront price before any work begins.
If the heater is under about 8 years old and the problem is a part, such as an element, thermostat, thermocouple, or valve, repair usually makes sense. If water is leaking from the tank body, the hot water runs rusty, or a tank unit is past 10 to 12 years old, replacement is the smarter investment, because a corroded tank cannot be patched. Our technicians walk you through both numbers honestly so you can decide with real information.
